Why small suppliers lose — and how 2026 changed the game

The old problem

Government RFPs run 100+ pages. Evaluators disqualify on a single missed mandatory criterion. Small firms' share of federal contract value has fallen to roughly half of what it was in 2008 — not because the work got worse, but because bidding got heavier.

The 2026 shift

The Buy Canadian Policy now favours Canadian suppliers, and the federal Small Business Procurement Program (July 2026) requires every purchase between $10K and $5M to be designed with small businesses in mind. Ontario and BC have matching rules. Buyers are actively looking for you.

What we bring

AI does the reading, cross-referencing, and first drafts in hours instead of weeks; an experienced human checks every requirement and every fact before it reaches you. That's how a $1,500 package matches work that used to cost ten times as much.

Questions contractors actually ask

Do you guarantee we'll win?

No — and be wary of anyone who does. We guarantee a compliant, professional, on-time package and an honest no-bid call when a tender isn't winnable for you. Your win rate is the number we track and report every quarter.

Is it really written for us, or is it AI boilerplate?

Every package is built from your company profile — your certifications, crews, past contracts, service area. Anything we don't know is flagged for you to confirm, never invented. Evaluators can spot boilerplate; so can we.

We've never bid on government work. Can we start from zero?

Yes — that's our Prequal Setup ($1,900 one-time): supplier registrations, safety documentation, insurance package, and your first tender watchlist, typically inside two weeks.

What about French-language tenders in Québec?

We deliver packages in English and French, including SEAO opportunities. Bilingual capability is a rated advantage on many federal tenders — we'll tell you when it applies.

Who sees our company information?

Your profile lives in our systems, is used only to prepare your bids, and is never shared. You can request its deletion at any time.
