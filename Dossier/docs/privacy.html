Dossier privacy policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Dossier is a home-report app for iOS and the web. This policy explains what the app does with information, which is very little.

What Dossier collects

Nothing. Dossier has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own. The app does not collect, transmit or sell personal information.

What stays on your device

Listing facts you type, the buyer profile you enter on the Find deals screen (income, debts, cash, household size, workplace), your listing pool, your checklist ticks and any reports you save are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them. Reports you choose to share leave the device only through the iOS share sheet, to the app or person you pick.

Network use

The iOS app works offline. It may load fonts from Google Fonts when a connection is available; Google's handling of that request is covered by Google's privacy policy. When you run the optional desktop server from the open-source project, a report can query public US government and OpenStreetMap services (Census geocoder, FEMA flood maps, USGS elevation, Census ACS, Overpass) with the address you entered; those requests go directly from the server you run to those services.

Children

Dossier is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/frizzyfrizwan/Story/issues.
